# Project 10 — Disease-Associated Mutations & Protein Structure Analysis

## Connecting genetic variation with protein structure and function

### Research Question

How can disease-associated mutations be investigated at the protein level to understand their possible structural and functional consequences?

---

## 1. Project Overview

Genetic mutations can alter protein sequences and potentially affect protein structure, stability, interactions or function.

This project introduces a structure-based approach to studying disease-associated mutations.

The analysis connects three levels of biological information:

Disease-associated mutation
        ↓
Protein sequence
        ↓
Protein structure
